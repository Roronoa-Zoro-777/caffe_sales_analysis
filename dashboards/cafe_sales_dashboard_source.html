Key insights

  • Salad leads with 21% of revenue, while Coffee has the most orders yet only 9% of revenue: price drives revenue, not popularity.
  • Units per item are almost equal (3.3K to 3.9K) across prices from $1 to $5, so Cookie, Tea and Coffee may be under-priced (test before acting).
  • Orders of 4 to 5 units bring 61% of revenue, which makes multi-buy bundles worth testing.
  • Months, weekdays, payment methods and locations are flat; order values do not differ significantly.
  • Data gaps are the biggest issue: 32% of transactions lack a payment method, 40% lack a location and 4.6% lack a date.
